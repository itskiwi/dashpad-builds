# Dashpad Companion privacy policy

Dashpad Companion is a personal app that links an Android phone to the Dashpad app on an iPad in the same car.

**What it uses, and where it goes**

- **Battery level and charging state**, **location (GPS position and speed)** and **whether a phone call is active** (not who is calling) are sent only to the Dashpad iPad on the phone's own Wi-Fi hotspot or local network. Each of these can be switched off in the app.
- Android Auto traffic between the phone and the iPad passes through the app on the phone itself, only to the iPad.
- Nothing is sent to the developer, to any server or to any third party. The app has no analytics, ads or accounts.
- Settings (the switches, the iPad's local address, the chosen look and a parking meter time) are kept only on the phone.

**Permissions**

- Location: to send GPS to the iPad for night mode and speed-based volume.
- Phone (call state): to tell the iPad when a call starts or ends so it can pause its own sound.
- Display over other apps: so the app can open Android Auto when the iPad asks for it.
- Notifications and a foreground service: to show that the link is running and to remind about a parking meter.
